1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & VISION
================================================================================
SmartSurplus Ecosystem is a next-generation smart logistics and circular economy 
platform built to solve two massive humanitarian and environmental challenges:
  1. Urban Hunger and Food Insecurity.
  2. Landfill Food Waste and Methane Greenhouse Gas Emissions.

SmartSurplus does not simply list surplus food on a bulletin board. It operates 
an intelligent multi-criteria decision engine that matches fresh surplus food to 
the closest, most capable, verified NGO for human consumption. If food cannot be 
collected before its safe consumption deadline, the platform automatically 
redirects it to the nearest verified Biogas Energy Facility, converting food waste 
into clean energy and bio-fertilizer, ensuring ZERO landfill waste.

================================================================================
2. THE PROBLEM STATEMENT & MARKET OPPORTUNITY
================================================================================
- More than 1/3rd of all food produced globally is wasted.
- Decomposing food in landfills produces Methane (CH4), a greenhouse gas 28 times 
  more potent than Carbon Dioxide (CO2).
- At the same time, millions suffer from nutritional poverty in urban centers.

Traditional donation platforms fail due to:
  * Slow manual matching leading to food spoilage before pickup.
  * Inefficient routing causing high food transit times.
  * Lack of capacity awareness (overwhelming small shelters while large NGOs sit idle).
  * No safety net when food expires, resulting in municipal landfill disposal.
  * Lack of trust and legal verification between donors and recipients.

SmartSurplus solves all five barriers through automated AI matching, real-time 
spoilage timers, verified entity checks, and automatic biogas diversion.

3. CORE DUAL-TRACK ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

[ TRACK 1: PRIMARY PATH - HUMAN REDISTRIBUTION ]
Food Donor creates surplus listing 
  -> Smart Matching Engine evaluates candidates in real-time
  -> Top-ranked verified NGO receives instant high-priority notification
  -> NGO accepts & dispatches volunteer/driver
  -> OTP-verified pickup and delivery handover
  -> Surplus food feeds hungry individuals & families.

[ TRACK 2: SECONDARY PATH - CIRCULAR CLEAN ENERGY CONVERSION ]
Food listing approaches safe consumption deadline without an NGO pickup
  -> Automated Spoilage Timer fires trigger
  -> System redirects listing to Nearest Verified Biogas Digester Plant
  -> Biogas logistics dispatches collection vehicle
  -> Organic waste is converted to compressed biogas (CBG) & organic compost
  -> Zero food reaches landfills.

4. COMPREHENSIVE SMART MATCHING ENGINE (PYTHON & JS AI MICROSERVICE)
================================================================================
SmartSurplus uses a deterministic Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA) 
Weighted Scoring Model implemented in Python (`python_matching_service.py`) 
and executed through Node.js (`matchingService.js`).

A. HARD CONSTRAINT PRE-FILTERING (Eligibility Gate):
   Before scoring, all NGOs in the database are filtered:
   1. `is_verified == 1` (Admin approved entity with valid legal registrations).
   2. `is_available == 1` (Actively open and ready for distribution).
   3. `ngo_capacity >= donation_quantity` (Must have physical storage capacity).
   4. Valid latitude and longitude coordinates present.

B. THE 5-FACTOR WEIGHTED SCORING FORMULA:
   Total Score (0 to 100) = 
       (Distance Score    x 0.25) +
       (Capacity Score    x 0.20) +
       (Urgency Score     x 0.25) +
       (Availability Score x 0.15) +
       (Response Score    x 0.15)

   1. Distance Score (25% Weight - Haversine Geographic Calculation):
      Measures exact straight-line distance in kilometers:
      - <= 2 km  : 100 points
      - <= 5 km  : 80 points
      - <= 10 km : 60 points
      - <= 20 km : 40 points
      - <= 30 km : 20 points
      - > 30 km  : 0 points

   2. Capacity Score (20% Weight):
      Evaluates whether the NGO can comfortably handle the quantity:
      - Ratio = NGO Food Capacity / Donation Quantity
      - If Ratio >= 2.0 : 100 points
      - If Ratio < 2.0  : Ratio * 50 points (e.g., 1.2 ratio = 60 points)

   3. Food Urgency Score (25% Weight):
      Evaluates remaining freshness window based on `safe_until` timestamp:
      - < 30 minutes remaining : 100 points (Critical immediate dispatch)
      - 30 to 60 minutes       : 80 points (High priority)
      - 60 to 120 minutes      : 60 points (Moderate priority)
      - > 120 minutes          : 40 points (Standard priority)

   4. Availability Score (15% Weight):
      Evaluates active operational status and readiness:
      - Active, open shelter status : 100 points (Full 15 points contribution)

   5. Response History Score (15% Weight):
      Evaluates historical reliability and acceptance rate:
      - Score = NGO historical response rate percentage (e.g., 95% = 95 points)
      - Defaults to 90.0% for new verified entities.

C. EXPLAINABLE AI OUTPUT:
   The engine generates structured, human-readable explanations:
   "✓ 3.4 km distance calculated via Python AI Model"
   "✓ Sufficient capacity (150 meals)"
   "✓ Active availability status"
   "✓ Response history score (95.0%)"

   - Access to uncollected/expired food listings redirected automatically.

4. SYSTEM ADMIN:
   - Entity legal verification (PAN, 12A/80G, FCRA, NGO Darpan IDs, Biogas licenses).
   - User account moderation and role adjustments.
   - Platform-wide telemetry (Total food rescued, CO2 avoided, active matches).

6. FOOD SAFETY TIMERS & AUTOMATED EXPIRY PREVENTION
================================================================================
To ensure microbiological safety, donations adhere to category shelf-life rules:
  - Cooked Gravy / Dairy Dishes : 2 Hours Maximum Window
  - Cooked Dry / Rice / Breads  : 4 Hours Maximum Window
  - Bakery & Confectionery      : 8 Hours Maximum Window
  - Packaged Foods / Fresh Veg  : 24 Hours Maximum Window

Background timers continuously evaluate active listings. If 30 minutes remain, 
high-priority visual warnings and notifications are broadcast. If the safe 
window elapses without pickup, Track 2 (Biogas Redirection) triggers instantly.

7. CIRCULAR ECONOMY & BIOGAS REDIRECTION LIFECYCLE
================================================================================
Status Progression for Expired Food:
  AVAILABLE -> EXPIRED -> REDIRECTED_TO_BIOGAS -> BIOGAS_ACCEPTED -> 
  BIOGAS_IN_TRANSIT -> BIOGAS_COLLECTED -> PROCESSED_INTO_ENERGY

Conversion Metrics:
  - 1 Ton Food Waste -> ~100 to 120 m³ Biogas (~60% Methane).
  - Equivalent to ~200 kWh Clean Electrical Energy or ~50 kg Bio-CNG fuel.
  - Generates nutrient-rich liquid digestate (organic fertilizer).

================================================================================

- Live marker visualization for Donor, NGO, Vehicle Driver, and Biogas Plant.
- Socket.IO WebSockets stream real-time coordinate updates and status shifts.

13. COMPLETE REST API REFERENCE
================================================================================
Authentication:
  POST /api/auth/register          - Register user (DONOR, NGO, BIOGAS)
  POST /api/auth/login             - Authenticate & receive JWT
  GET  /api/auth/me                - Get current logged-in user profile

Donations & Matching:
  POST /api/donations              - Create new surplus food listing
  GET  /api/donations              - List donations (filtered by role/status)
  GET  /api/donations/:id          - Get specific donation details
  POST /api/matching/match/:id     - Trigger Python Smart Matching Engine
  POST /api/matching/accept/:id    - NGO accepts matched donation

Logistics & Verification:
  POST /api/donations/verify-otp   - Verify pickup or delivery OTP
  GET  /api/tracking/:id           - Get live GPS coordinates & transit steps

Biogas & Waste:
  GET  /api/biogas/requests        - View redirected organic waste requests
  POST /api/biogas/process/:id     - Log completed biogas digestion batch

Admin & Impact:
  GET  /api/admin/organizations    - List pending verification organizations
  PUT  /api/admin/verify/:id       - Approve / Reject entity verification

15. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (JUDGE & PRESENTATION READY)
================================================================================
Q: How does SmartSurplus differ from standard food donation apps?
A: SmartSurplus is the first platform with an automated closed-loop dual-track 
   system. It combines rule-based AI multi-criteria matching with an automated 
   biogas redirection safety net, guaranteeing zero food reaches landfills.

Q: Why not use a Large Language Model (LLM) for matching?
A: Matching requires strict mathematical accuracy, zero hallucinations, instant 
   sub-10ms latency, and 100% explainable auditing. Our Python MCDA heuristic 
   provides guaranteed precision without cloud API costs or latency.

Q: How is food safety enforced?
A: Dynamic time-decay timers tailored to specific food categories automatically 
   disallow human matching if the food passes its biological consumption threshold.
